## Coverage and interpretation limits

There are 732 normal cash-session dates in the development interval; 653 have eligible
lagged ATR. Initial warmup and missing historical bars remove the remaining dates.
One gap can invalidate multiple subsequent ATR observations. Calendar, coverage and
filter exclusions mean this is not a claim that every date produced a valid trade.

40,904 configurations produced no trades. 216,312 configurations have at least one
unknown signal/trade path and must not be promoted as clean. Every noise-momentum
configuration has such a flag. Its zero screen qualifiers therefore does not prove
the mechanism has no edge: data quality is a material blocker.

The 568,296 entries are parameter combinations, not independent strategies or evidence
samples. Some settings yield the same behavior (for example, a breakeven rule triggered
at 1R cannot help a trade already exited at its 1R target). Filters can also be redundant.
393,594 distinct reported metric rows remain after ignoring ID, family and intended
risk; this is only a descriptive count, not a statistical effective-trial estimate.

## Two pitfalls caught before ranking

**Account SURVIVED does not mean the account kept trading or passed an evaluation.**
An account can remain above its loss floor but have too little room to size another
trade. For example, ORB_OPEN_TREND `R2-0445806` reports raw-strategy profit of $13,174,
but its account path ends at $48,500.50, with its last trade on August 4, 2023 and 346
size skips (versus seven in the raw strategy). It is excluded from the preferred list.
The simulator does not claim evaluation targets, payouts or funded scaling were met.

**The inherited mechanical screen has no positive-profit requirement.** Its 881
qualifiers include 77 losing configurations. Only 804 are profitable. Overnight drift
has eight mechanical qualifiers, all losing; it is not a successful family here.

The analysis retains the frozen outputs and applies explicit extra filters separately.
It does not rewrite the original screen or hide its failures.

### Neighborhoods, account starts and overlap

At fixed $300 risk, vary exactly one coordinate to another value in the declared grid.
These are categorical one-coordinate comparisons, not uniformly spaced local tests.
The gap-fade candidate has 15/18 profitable neighbors (five pass the original screen);
closing momentum has 9/12 (five pass); opening direction has 15/15 (seven pass).
Profitable neighbors do not imply all neighbors meet drawdown or data requirements.

Twelve quarterly account start dates from 2023–2025 were replayed through the same
development endpoint for each candidate. None of these five candidates added sizing
skips relative to its raw strategy. Gap fade's account sizing changed P&L for two
starts; the other four matched raw P&L for all twelve. These overlapping, unequal-length
windows are a diagnostic only, not twelve independent tests or an evaluation pass rate.

Daily P&L correlation on all 732 normal development dates is approximately -0.001
between gap fade and closing momentum, -0.034 between opening direction and closing
momentum, and +0.383 between the two ORB candidates. Different trading times and
sparse trading contribute to this. No combined-account allocation, margin/netting,
hedging-policy or portfolio risk test has been completed.

## Comparison with Research 1 and decision

Research 1's preferred ORB09 C047761 remains the recorded reference: $18,823.50,
PF 1.774, DD $1,339, 291 trades. The largest profit among these Research 2 candidates
is lower. None automatically displaces that shortlist.

Direct numerical ranking across studies is premature: entry/exit conventions, stops,
calendar/data coverage, sizing and account treatment differ. The nominal commissions
match, but fill treatment differs. Research 1's reported Sharpe uses trade-active
days; Research 2's Sharpe includes all normal development dates. Do not compare those
Sharpe values as if they use the same definition. A common-data/common-engine replay
must precede a claim that one study's model is superior.

All five surviving family leaders use the widest ATR stop tested (20% of daily ATR).
That is a grid-boundary warning, not proof of a globally optimal setting. Any broader
stop experiment must be versioned and counted as additional research trials.

**Decision:** retain Research 1's provisional shortlist; advance the first three
Research 2 candidates to common-engine and independent-data validation, with two reserves.
Repair/replace missing data before judging noise momentum conclusively. Do not promote
zero-qualifier families on raw profit, treat a stalled account as a successful strategy,
or describe a completed parameter grid as live-ready.
